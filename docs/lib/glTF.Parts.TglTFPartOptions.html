Settings that decide which bones a part covers and what it replaces.

Start from TglTFPartOptions.Default and change what you need.

Description

Fields

Public CoverGap: Double;

How close a part's surface must come to the body for the body to be hidden there, as a share of the body's height; 0.03 by default.

A body vertex is covered when a part's surface crosses the line along its normal no further than this from it, on either side: outside the skin, or inside it where the body pokes through the cloth. A triangle is hidden when its three corners are covered. Larger values hide the body under looser clothes too; smaller ones keep more of it, at the risk of skin showing through.

Public MinShare: Double;

Share of a part's triangles a bone must drive for the part to cover that bone, from 0 to 1; 0.02 by default.

The bones a part covers decide which parts it replaces (see ReplaceShare) and are listed for it; they do not decide what of the body is hidden (see CoverGap).

Public ReplaceShare: Double;

Share of a part's triangles on the bones a new part covers for the new part to replace it, from 0 to 1; 0.5 by default.

Only parts put on earlier are replaced; the body and the model's other meshes are hidden where covered instead.

Public KeepBones: TArray<string>;

Bones never covered, compared ignoring case; none by default.

Body vertices whose strongest bone is one of these are never hidden, and parts do not replace each other on account of these bones. For example Head keeps the whole head under a hood.
