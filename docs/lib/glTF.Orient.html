Unit glTF.Orient

Description
Classes, Interfaces, Objects and Records

Description

Re-orients a whole glTF model: turns it about the X, Y and Z axes and bakes the turn into the data.

Models are often exported with a different up or forward axis from the one an engine expects, and are then given a starting rotation in the engine. TglTFReorienter does that turn once, in the file: the model saved afterwards is the right way up, with nothing extra to apply.

The turn is X degrees about the X axis, then Y about the Y axis, then Z about the Z axis, each counter-clockwise looking down the axis towards the origin, as usual in glTF's right-handed space. For example X = -90 turns a Z-up model into glTF's Y-up.

Every vertex ends up exactly where the turn takes it, and so does everything else:

  • Mesh data: positions, normals and tangents, and the same in morph targets, are turned; the min and max of the accessors that have them are computed again.

  • Nodes: each node's transform R L R-1 keeps the hierarchy as it was, turned as a whole; the root nodes take the turn itself.

  • Animations: translation, rotation and scale key frames are turned the same way as the transforms they animate, including CUBICSPLINE tangents.

  • Skins: the inverse bind matrices are turned to match.

  • Cameras and lights, which look down their node's -Z axis, keep their node's turn, so they still point the same way in the turned model.

Winding and handedness do not change, as a rotation is not a mirror. A non-uniform scale can only be turned exactly by a multiple of 90 degrees; otherwise the node becomes a matrix, or a warning is given when its scale is animated. Quantized data (KHR_mesh_quantization) is turned and quantized again. Positions given by the EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing extension are not turned; a warning says so.
