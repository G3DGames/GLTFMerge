Unit glTFViewer.Parts

Description
Classes, Interfaces, Objects and Records

Description

The Parts tab of the viewer: body parts and outfit pieces to put on the model, and those on it.

TglTFPartsPanel has two lists. The upper one, "Parts loaded", shows every part found in the part files loaded (see glTF.Parts), one row each with the columns Use, Part, File, Triangles, Covers and Status; Covers lists the bones the part covers with their shares. The lower one, "Parts on the model", shows the parts put on the model, with the columns Take off, Node, Part, From, Triangles and Covers.

"Add part files..." raises TglTFPartsPanel.OnAddFiles, "Put on ticked parts" TglTFPartsPanel.OnApply and "Take off ticked parts" TglTFPartsPanel.OnRemove; the main form does the work and then calls RefreshList. The settings row decides how much of the body is hidden under the parts: how close a part's surface must come to the skin, as a share of the body's height, and the bones never hidden. "Use on the model" hides the body again with them, through TglTFPartsPanel.OnRefreshModel; otherwise they apply from the next change.

On narrow screens the File and From columns are hidden, and Triangles too when narrower still. The panel is built in code, so the unit needs no .fmx file; headings and rows are laid out by glTFViewer.Columns, so the columns line up.
